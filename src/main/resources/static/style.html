Style & Label Management

Choose how every layer is drawn — one colour, a colour per class, a band per range or a graduated ramp — along with its line weight and pattern, its symbol, its outline and the text printed beside it. This is the layer's standing look: it is what the map draws on every open. The viewer's own colour by attribute selector stays a temporary view on top of it and still works as it always has, until the next reload. Nothing here changes until you save, and Reset to built-in always puts a layer back exactly as the viewer draws it out of the box. Road condition, PCI and the 2 km IRI roll-up are coloured from their own screen in the viewer, by survey parameter and threshold, so they are not listed here.
