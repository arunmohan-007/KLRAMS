Folders & layers

Every layer belongs to one of three groups. System Generated layers are rebuilt automatically from survey data. Protected Layers are the core KLRAMS inventory — neither can be removed. Only layers under Administrative Boundary & Custom Layers can be created, hidden, or removed.

New folder

Folders you create here live under Administrative Boundary & Custom Layers — they can always be hidden, and layers inside them can always be removed.

New layer

Pick a Boundary or Condition folder. Every layer created here is added under Administrative Boundary & Custom Layers and can be hidden at any time — Protected Layers and System Generated folders are fixed by the system.

KLRAMS stores every layer in WGS 1984 (EPSG:4326) — the same projection used across the GIS viewer, tiles, and every existing table. This is fixed, not editable.
